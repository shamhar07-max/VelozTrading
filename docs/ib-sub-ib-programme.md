# IB & Sub-IB Programme — Master Specification

**Document ID:** VEL-IB-SPEC-2026-R1 · **Date:** 23 August 2026 · **Classification:** Confidential — Internal Use Only

Single authoritative specification of the VelozTrade Introducing Broker (IB) and Sub-IB programme.
Supersedes VEL-HR-2026-0811-R2 §5–6 and VEL-IBOPS-2026-0811-R1 on points of conflict. Grounded in the
production schema: `partners`, `referrals`, `partner_commissions`, `accounts`, `transactions`.

## Design principles

| Principle | In practice |
|---|---|
| Attribution is permanent | A client introduced by an IB belongs to that IB's book forever. Re-attribution requires written consent of both IBs and the Sole Proprietor. |
| No payout without reconciliation | Commissions accrue automatically but are released only after the monthly run reconciles and the Sole Proprietor authorises payment. |
| KYC gates everything | No commission withdrawal before the partner's own KYC is approved; no CPA before the referred client's qualifying (≥ $250) deposit settles. |
| Dual authorisation on funds | Every payout requires system calculation plus human approval; no single actor moves funds alone. |
| Everything is auditable | Each commission line carries its source (position, referral, adjustment); any payout traces back to originating trades. |

## 1 · Roles & definitions

| Role | Definition | Platform identity |
|---|---|---|
| Sole Proprietor | Ultimate counterparty of every IB agreement; approves onboarding, sets rates, authorises payouts, resolves disputes. Final sign-off is non-delegable. | Admin role (`requireAdmin`) |
| Introducing Broker (IB) | Independent person/entity introducing clients under a signed IB Agreement; services its book; may sponsor sub-IB desks. | `partners` row, `parentPartnerId = null` |
| Sub-IB (desk) | Downstream introducer beneath exactly one parent IB; own clients and earnings; parent earns flat override. May not sponsor further tiers. | `partners` row with non-null `parentPartnerId` |
| Referred client | Trader permanently attributed to the partner whose code was used at signup. | `accounts.referred_by_partner_id` + `referrals` row |

**Hard limit — two tiers only.** A sub-IB can never create a sub-sub-IB. Links generated by a sub-IB
attribute clients to the sub-IB itself; the parent earns only its override. Enforced by service-layer
invariant: `parent_partner_id` may only reference a partner whose own parent is null.

## 2 · Eligibility & onboarding

**Who qualifies**

- **IB:** individual or registered entity with a demonstrable trader network/audience; clean compliance record; no history of fund-handling complaints.
- **Sub-IB:** proposed by a parent IB in good standing; same vetting plus written sponsorship.
- **All:** full KYC verification before any commission withdrawal; one partner account per natural person or legal entity.

**Workflow**

| Step | Stage | Action & owner | SLA / gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Application | Candidate submits profile: entity type, markets, audience size, channels, sample material. | — |
| 2 | Vetting | Compliance reviews identity, adverse media, marketing practices. | ≤ 48h decision |
| 3 | Commercial terms | Sole Proprietor fixes `cpa_rate`, `rev_share_pct`, lot-rebate tier, seeded capital (IBs only). | Offer sheet |
| 4 | Agreement | E-sign IB/Sub-IB Agreement (+ parent-linkage addendum for sub-IBs). | Gate: no agreement, no code |
| 5 | Provisioning | Admin creates `partners` row: unique code (`VT-IB-xx-nnn` / `VT-SUB-xx-nnn`), parent linkage, tier, seeded capital; links own trading account via `clerk_user_id`. | Same day as signature |
| 6 | KYC completion | Partner completes in-platform KYC. | Gate: `status='active'` only after approval |
| 7 | Enablement pack | Referral link + QR, dashboard walkthrough, marketing rulebook, escalation contacts. | Day 1 post-activation |

**Referral attribution mechanics**

1. Permanent links (`/sign-up?ref=CODE`) and QR codes — no cookie windows, no expiry.
2. Signup stamps the client account with the partner id and creates a `referrals` row (`deposit_status='none'`).
3. When cumulative deposits reach ≥ $250, `deposit_status` → `'qualified'`; CPA payable at next run (`cpa_paid` prevents double payment).
4. Retroactive attribution allowed only within 7 days of signup via admin tooling with audit note; attribution frozen afterwards.

## 3 · Earning architecture — four streams

| Stream | Who earns | Basis | Accrual timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| A · Lot rebate | IB & sub-IB | Fixed USD per standard lot traded by referred clients, tiered by monthly network volume. | Per closed trade, nightly batch |
| B · Parent override | Parent IB | Flat USD per lot on all sub-IB client volume regardless of sub-IB tier. | Per closed trade, nightly batch |
| C · CPA bonus | IB & sub-IB | One-time payment on referred client's first qualifying deposit (≥ $250). Per-partner `cpa_rate`. | On qualification, monthly release |
| D · Revenue share | IB (negotiable for sub-IB) | % of platform net revenue (spread + commission) from referred client activity. Per-partner `rev_share_pct`. | Month-end on net revenue report |

**Standard lot-rebate grid (USD / standard lot)**

| Tier | Network monthly volume | IB rate | Sub-IB rate | Parent override | Volume rebate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 0 – 500 lots | $8.00 | $5.00 | $3.00 / lot on all sub-IB client volume | $0.10 / lot on first 1,000 lots monthly |
| Tier 2 | 501 – 1,500 lots | $9.50 | $6.50 | ↑ | ↑ |
| Tier 3 | 1,501+ lots | $11.00 | $8.00 | ↑ | ↑ |

Tier assignment uses previous calendar month's settled network volume and holds constant during the
current month. Volumes round down to whole lots; micro-lots accrue fractionally at 1/100 rate.

**Worked example (month):** sub-IB at Tier 1 with 400 lots earns 400 × $5.00 + 400 × $0.10 = **$2,040**;
its parent earns 400 × $3.00 = **$1,200** override, plus its own direct-client earnings separately.

**Seeded capital & milestone unlocks (IBs only, optional):** platform allocates trading capital
(`seeded_capital`); principal stays platform-owned until unlocked; profits flow to `commission_wallet`
immediately at 100%. Unlock by lifetime depositing referrals: 10 → 25%, 25 → 50%, 50 → 75%, 100 → 100%.
Percentage never decreases; principal withdrawal only for unlocked portions after KYC + dual authorisation.

## 4 · Money flow — trade to bank account

```
client trade closes → nightly accrual (02:00 UTC) → month-end run (1st biz day)
→ reconciliation (by 3rd biz day) → proprietor approval → payout ≤ 48h of request
```

1. **Trade close** — atomic source event carrying position id + client id.
2. **Nightly accrual** — batch job computes lot rebates, overrides, rev-share estimates; writes immutable rows into `partner_commissions` with `source_type ∈ {lot_rebate, parent_override, cpa, rev_share, adjustment}`. Visible as *pending* immediately.
3. **Month-end run** — aggregate per partner, apply clawbacks, validate tier, produce Commission Run Report.
4. **Reconciliation** — three manual spot checks per region against raw trades; zero variance tolerance; discrepancies freeze the affected partner's run and escalate within 1 hour.
5. **Approval** — line-by-line or bulk; approval transfers aggregates into `commission_wallet` with an audit entry.
6. **Payout** — on request, min $10, via bank wire, crypto (BTC/ETH/USDT), Skrill/Neteller, JazzCash, Easypaisa, UPI; ≤ 24–48h processing. Requires partner KYC verified/approved; posts through the immutable ledger.

**Clawbacks & adjustments**

| Event | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Client withdraws ≥ 80% of deposited funds within 30 days of CPA qualification | CPA reversed next run (negative `adjustment` referencing original CPA row). |
| Trade voided by admin (error/abuse/anomaly) | Linked commission reversed via `adjustment` pointing at same `ref_position_id`. |
| Fraud / self-referral / incentivised signup confirmed | Commissions from offending referrals withheld; partner suspended pending review. |
| Manual goodwill/commercial adjustment | Sole Proprietor only, mandatory free-text reason, shown separately in run report. |

## 5 · Platform data model

| Table | Programme role |
|---|---|
| `partners` | One row per IB/sub-IB: unique `referral_code`, `parent_partner_id` (null ⇒ IB), commercial terms (`cpa_rate`, `rev_share_pct`, `tier`), seeded-capital state, live `commission_wallet`, `status`, `legacy_id` traceability. |
| `referrals` | Permanent attribution edge; unique (partner, client); `deposit_status` progression `none → qualified`; `cpa_paid` guard. |
| `partner_commissions` | Append-only earning-event ledger; `source_type` + originating position/client references. |
| `transactions` / `accounts` | Wallet credits/debits post through the same immutable ledger discipline as client balances. |

**Service-layer invariants**

- `parent_partner_id` may only point at a top-level partner (no grandchild desks).
- New accruals require `status='active'`; suspended partners keep historical rows but accrue nothing.
- CPA pays at most once per (partner, client): unique pair + `cpa_paid`.
- `capital_unlocked_pct` transitions are monotonic; decreases rejected.
- Every wallet mutation mirrors into `transactions` inside the same DB transaction.

## 6 · Operating rhythm

- **Daily:** 08:00 accrual health check (job success, rows vs closed positions); 08:30 deposit/withdrawal queue incl. pending partner payouts vs SLA; 17:00 accrued-commissions vs closed-volume reconciliation, zero unexplained variance.
- **Weekly (Fri):** sub-IB conduct sampling; KPI watch vs plan; liquidity & wallet checks; summary broadcast to partners by 18:00 GMT.
- **Monthly:** commission run; statements published; tier recomputation; per-IB performance review (amber/red); seeded-capital milestone updates.
- **Quarterly:** full book audit per partner (complaints, clawback ratio, concentration); agreement re-affirmation; strategy review.

Escalation constants: unexplained fund movement, outage, margin-call cluster, fraud suspicion, or complaint above desk authority ⇒ notify Sole Proprietor within 1 hour. Amber flag ⇒ coaching call within 48h. Red flag ⇒ immediate suspension review.

## 7 · Compliance & conduct rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| KYC before money out | Partner withdrawals blocked unless KYC verified/approved; corporates add UBO declarations. |
| No handling of client funds | Partners never accept cash/personal/third-party payments for clients; all funding flows platform-direct. Violation = immediate termination. |
| Marketing standards | No guaranteed-profit or "risk-free" claims; no brand bidding; no undisclosed incentivised signups; risk warning mandatory on public claims. |
| Self-referral prohibition | Partner's own accounts earn nothing; matched by identity/KYC signals; triggers review. |
| Data privacy | Masked client identifiers in partner dashboards; no scraping/exporting/cross-marketing of client lists. |
| Programme changes | Material commission changes need 30 days written notice; no unilateral mid-month reductions. |

## 8 · Lifecycle management

| State | Entry condition | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| applied → vetting | Application submitted | No code issued; nothing accruable. |
| active | Agreement signed + KYC approved + provisioned | Link live, accruals on, dashboard enabled. |
| amber (flag) | < 80% plan or minor conduct warning | Coaching call ≤ 48h; weekly monitoring. |
| suspended | Red-flag review / fraud investigation / breach remediation | Link disabled; accruals halted; wallet frozen; client book ring-fenced (clients keep trading). |
| terminated | Breach, fraud confirmed, or mutual exit | Unpaid earned commissions settled minus lawful clawbacks; book reassignment decided by Sole Proprietor (sub-IB books usually to parent IB). |
| dormant | No referred activity 90 days | Flag only; wallet withdrawable; reactivates on next qualified activity. |

**Continuity guarantee:** client books are platform assets, not partner property. Termination never
interrupts client trading, balances, or open positions; attribution transfers administratively.

## 9 · KPIs & review framework

| KPI | Definition | Green | Amber | Red |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Network volume | Lots/month vs plan | ≥ 95% | 80–94% | < 80% |
| Depositing referrals | New qualified clients/month vs plan | ≥ plan | 80–99% | < 80% |
| Clawback ratio | Reversed ÷ gross commissions (rolling 3m) | < 3% | 3–7% | > 7% |
| Complaint rate | Verified complaints per 100 active clients | < 1 | 1–3 | > 3 |
| Conduct samples | Marketing/acquisition audits passed | 100% | ≥ 90% | < 90% |

## 11 · Governance & change control

- **Owner:** Sole Proprietor — sole authority over onboarding, termination, rate changes, payout release.
- This document is version-controlled; amendments require a new revision ID with change log; material changes notified to partners per §7.
- **Audit rights:** every commission line reconstructable from raw trades within one business day; quarterly internal audit verifies a random 10% partner sample.

*Standard terms above; a signed partner annex may vary individual terms and prevails where recorded.*
