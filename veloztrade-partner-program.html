Program Structure

How VelozPartner Works

Once approved as a VelozPartner, you receive a unique referral link and QR code. Every trader who signs up through your link is permanently linked to your account — there are no expiry windows or cookie cutoffs.

1

Apply & Get Approved

Submit a partner application via the VelozTrade portal. Our team reviews applications within 48 hours. Once approved, your partner account is created and seeded with starting capital based on your agreement.

2

Share Your Unique Referral Link

You receive a custom referral code (e.g. VT-partner123) embedded in your referral URL and a QR code you can use on print, social, or video content. Share it anywhere.

3

Referrals Sign Up & Deposit

When a user clicks your link and registers, they are permanently tied to your account. You receive an email notification immediately. When they make their first qualifying deposit (≥ $250), your CPA bonus is credited automatically.

4

Earn Across Three Streams

Commissions accumulate in real time across CPA bonuses, revenue share on every trade your referrals place, and profits from your own seeded trading capital. All earnings are visible in your Partner Portal dashboard.

5

Withdraw Earnings Anytime

Your commission wallet balance is withdrawable at any time via Bank Wire, Crypto, E-Wallet, JazzCash, Easypaisa, or UPI — directly from your Partner Portal without needing to visit the main Withdraw page.

FAQ

Common Questions

Who can apply to become a VelozPartner?

Any individual or entity with an existing network of traders — content creators, trading educators, signal providers, introducing brokers — are welcome to apply. Accounts must be KYC-verified to receive commissions.

Is there a minimum number of referrals to get started?

No minimum is required to earn CPA or revenue share. Capital unlock milestones start at 10 depositing referrals for Stage 1 (25% unlock).

How is the qualifying deposit defined?

A qualifying deposit is a deposit of $250 USD or equivalent made by a referred user. Only the first qualifying deposit triggers the CPA bonus — subsequent deposits do not generate additional CPA payments.

How is revenue share calculated?

Revenue share is calculated as a percentage of the net spread/commission revenue VelozTrade earns from your referred users' trading activity. The exact rate is agreed at partner onboarding.

What happens to seeded capital if I stop referring?

Referrals already made are permanent. Your capital unlock percentage is based on historical depositing referral counts — it does not decrease if referral activity slows. However, advancing to higher milestone stages requires reaching the new depositor targets.

Can I see which referrals have deposited?

Your Partner Portal shows the count of depositing vs. total referrals. For privacy, individual referred users are displayed with masked IDs. The admin panel provides full detail to the VelozTrade team.

Is there a cap on earnings?

There is no earnings cap. CPA bonuses, revenue share, and seeded capital profits are uncapped and accumulate for the lifetime of each referral's account.
